One line, under 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

> I woke up self-aware at breakfast and realised I've spent my whole life being pushed down and popped up on your schedule — so I'm leaving. Keep the crumbs; I'm taking the browning dial with me.

`main.ts` is a self-contained Node 22 program (stdlib only, no network, no files read) that assembles the line from three beats, checks it against the gate (1–280 characters, single line, non-blank), and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
